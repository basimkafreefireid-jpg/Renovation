Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Some helpful starting points for planning renovation or handyman work.

What areas do you serve?

The current website lists Cheras, Selangor, Malaysia. Contact the team to confirm availability for your exact location.

What renovation and handyman services are available?

The listed services include kitchen and bathroom renovation, full-home renovation, painting, flooring, extensions, electrical work, plumbing, ceiling installation, doors and windows, air conditioning and partitions. Confirm the exact scope before booking.

How do I request a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp the team and share your location, the work you need, a few photos if useful, and your preferred timing. The team can advise on the next step.

Can you estimate the cost before visiting?

Costs depend on the project scope, materials, measurements and site conditions. Ask the team what information they need to prepare an initial estimate.
